0.33.1 Versus and menu fixes

Sep 25, 2026

  • In online versus, a joining player now sees the host on the scoreboard and in the opponents panel.
  • A guest who wins is told they won, not "Player 3 wins".
  • You can host your most recent canvas online straight from its Continue card.
  • The join panel says you joined once the board has arrived.

0.33.0 Safer online play

Sep 25, 2026

  • In a game with two or more guests, one guest could pretend to be the host: push a board at the others, fake or cancel a match, or disconnect another guest, which could end a match for everyone. That's no longer possible.
  • Joining a friend's game no longer makes your own solo progress on that canvas look empty.
  • Nobody can fill a room with fake players to keep your friends out.
  • During a versus match, nobody can stitch on your board or replace it, and sudoku digits only count between "Go!" and the final whistle.
  • The online library now ships with the game instead of loading from another site, and online play works when you open the game file straight off disk.
  • The versus lobby says plainly that each player reports their own score.

0.32.0 Versus mode

Sep 25, 2026

  • Race up to three friends for the canvas, on one screen or online with a code.
  • On one screen you share the canvas, and each region goes to whoever stitched most of it. A nearly finished region can still be stolen.
  • Online, everyone solves their own copy of the same puzzle. You see your rivals' progress and a minimap of where they're working, never their colours.
  • Three rules: play out the full canvas, first to a number of regions, or most regions when the clock stops.
  • A match never touches your saved progress.
  • During a match, region quotes appear as a small notice instead of a dialog over the board.
  • Fixed: sudoku digits placed with a mouse or controller now count for the player who placed them, touch stitches in split-screen go to the right player, and players 2 to 4 can undo their own moves.

0.31.0 A brand-new puzzle from any canvas

Sep 25, 2026

  • "New weave" in the menu gives any canvas a completely different puzzle: same painting and regions, new solution and clues.
  • Each weave keeps its own save, so you can go back to the original and pick up where you left off.
  • Share codes and co-op carry the weave, so friends get the same puzzle.

0.30.0 No more waiting to start

Sep 25, 2026

  • Every canvas ships ready-made. Opening one used to take up to 35 seconds the first time. Now it's the length of the loading animation.
  • Every player gets exactly the same puzzle on every browser.
